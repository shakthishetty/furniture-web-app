1️⃣ Manufacturer Management Section

Menu Item on Left Panel:
The menu item is labeled "Manufacturers" (with icon).
Clicking it opens the Manufacturer Management section.

New Button:
Add a button labeled "Add Manufacturer" at the top of the Manufacturer Management area.

Manufacturer Form Modal:
On clicking "Add Manufacturer", open a modal form that collects:

Manufacturer Name (required)

Address (required)

Email (required, valid format)

Phone Number (required)

Description (optional)

Photo Upload (image file, required)

Form Submission Behavior:

Upon submitting the form:

Save the manufacturer data into the database.

Display the newly created manufacturer in a table of manufacturers within the Manufacturer Management section, showing key details (e.g., Name, Address).

Manufacturer Details View:

Clicking a manufacturer in the table should open a detailed view showing all stored fields, including the uploaded photo.

Menu Item on Left Panel:
The menu item is labeled "Manufacturing" (with icon).

Assign Manufacturer to Order:

For each order row in the table:

Clicking it should open a modal that lists all existing manufacturers from the database.

After selecting a manufacturer and confirming:

Store the manufacturer assignment in the database for that specific order.

Display the assigned manufacturer’s name in the table under the relevant column.
